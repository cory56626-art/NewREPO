==Description==
The '''Brawler''' is a hardcore melee tower that attacks enemies with its mechanical fists. It can be purchased from the store for {{Gem|1,250}} and has a level requirement of {{Level|50}}.

The '''Brawler''' attacks enemies with its mechanical fists, dealing damage. At Level 2, it gains the ability to combo its attacks, making it attack enemies in a burst of three continuous attacks, which ends in a cooldown. The burst does not restart if there are no more enemies in its range. For example, if it lands two hits on enemies and stops attacking, the next hit will be a final hit. The final hit from a combo will deal increased damage and knock back enemies. The enemy is not always pushed back the same distance, as this is determined by its size. Larger enemies will be pushed back less than an average-sized enemy. Knockback has a 3 second long cooldown before it can be applied again.

The '''Brawler''' also has a unique ability where it can change its current position into one which the player can select. However, this ability has a cooldown of thirty seconds. Upon landing, it will create a shockwave that deals a decent amount of damage to enemies and knocks them back. Uniquely, the ''Reposition'' ability can damage [[Flying]] enemies.

The '''Brawler''' is considered an all-round tower due to its cheap placement cost, good DPS, and ability to knockback enemies from Level 2+.

The '''Brawler''' has a placement limit of ten per player. This is decreased to six in [[PVP]] mode.

==Strategy==
*The '''Brawler''' can be an efficient tower early on during the game.
**For hordes of weaker enemies, a few Level 0 '''Brawlers''' can quite efficiently clear out hordes of enemies.
**For singular, high health enemies, the Level 2 '''Brawler''' can be effective at killing them and slowing them down, giving your '''Brawlers''' and other towers more time to kill it.
*The '''Brawler''' at Level 2+ knocks back on the third hit, making it useful at slowing down enemies.
**Enemies immune to stun are not affected, as [[Stun Immunity]] prevents them from being knocked back. Additionally, larger enemies will be pushed back less than average-sized enemies.
**At Level 4, it gains the ''Reposition'' ability, which allows it to knock back enemies in a large area,
**If there is only a single targetable enemy, the '''Brawler''' is capable of constantly knocking it back, preventing it from progressing further.
*The '''Brawler''' has lead detection at Level 0, making it effective against lead enemies.
*The '''Brawler''' cannot be stunned by any enemy, making it quite useful against enemies like the [[Warden (Legacy Enemy)|Warden]] or [[Fallen Guardian]].
*Due to the '''Brawler's''' ''Reposition'' ability, it is likely that it will often get out of range of buffing towers, making them much less effective.
*The '''Brawler's''' ''Reposition'' ability, starting from Level 4, can easily take out hordes of weaker enemies.
**The ability can also hit [[Flying]] enemies, making '''Brawler''' quite effective for taking out hordes of [[Balloon]]s and [[Balloon Kid]]s faster.
**Also do note that this ability has a cooldown of 30 seconds, and [[Flying]] enemies will not be knocked back.
*Due to its pretty high DPS, [[Stun Immunity]] and the ''Reposition'' ability, the '''Brawler''' can actually do well against major bosses like the [[Molten Warlord]] unlike most other low range towers.
*The '''Brawler''' has a small placement footprint, allowing it to squeeze in beside other towers easily.
*Unlike other melee towers, the '''Brawler''' is not hitbox reliant, which means it can fully utilize [[Range Buff]]s.

==Trivia==
*The '''Brawler''' is the first tower that can change its position on the map without being sold, the second being [[Pursuit]].
*The '''Brawler''' is canonically [[Engineer]]'s brother.
*The '''Brawler''' is the cheapest tower to unlock with {{Gem|Gems}}.
*The '''Brawler''' trains with the [[Assassin]] and [[Slasher]], as revealed in [[Blind Faith]].
*The '''Brawler''' level 4 upgrade name; ''GALVAKNUCKLES'', is a reference to the [[w:c:callofduty:Galvaknuckles|Galvaknuckles]] from the ''Call of Duty'' series.
*The Level 5 upgrade name is a reference to [[w:c:callofduty:Staff of Lightning|Boreas' Fury]] from the ''Call of Duty'' series.
