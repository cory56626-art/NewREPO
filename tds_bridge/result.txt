==Description==
The '''Damage Buff''' is a buff that increases a tower's damage, making them stronger. It is displayed on the tower itself with the icon of an arm with muscle.

==Mechanics==
'''Damage Buff''' range reduction is calculated as:
::<big>'''Damage × (1 + Damage Buff)'''</big>
:::where '''Damage Buff''' is the debuff supplied in decimal form (for example, 10% is 0.1).

The damage increased by the '''Damage Buff''' will always round down to the nearest integer. For example, a tower that deals 5 damage with a 50% '''Damage Buff''' will deal 7 damage, instead of 7.5.

Multiple towers are not fully affected by the buff. A full list of towers affected fully, partially or that are unaffected can be found below.
