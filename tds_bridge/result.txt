==Description==
The '''Medic''' is an intermediate support tower that provides various buffs to other towers and replenishes base health after it has been lost. It can be unlocked from the store for {{Coins|2,000}}.

The '''Medic''' heals base health by a specified amount each wave with ''Take your Vitamins''. The amount of health healed increases with the level of the '''Medic''' and the amount of '''Medics''' placed down. It also has the ability to overheal, increasing base health to value higher than its maximum. If there are multiple '''Medics''', the health regeneration and overheal stacks with each '''Medic'''. Similarly, these effects stack with other sources of such effects, like [[Skills]].

The '''Medic''' holds a medi gun, which it can use to apply a shield to towers. Whenever shielded, the tower will have a shield modifier on them. This shield provides a passive 15% [[Firerate Buff]] and an immunity to stuns. It does not grant an immunity to debuffs or freeze. If the tower is stunned, its shield will be broken instead, reappearing shortly after. At Level 0, it can only shield one tower, but this is increased up to five at Level 5. It cannot shield other '''Medics''' or any tower currently shielded by another '''Medic'''. There is a 0.5 second cooldown before another tower can be selected or deselected.

At Level 3, it gets the '''Ubercharge''' ability. This provides all towers shielded by the '''Medic''' with a [[Damage Buff]], [[Stun Immunity]] and [[Freeze Immunity]] for a brief period of time. While it may look like this is tied to the shield, this is not actually the case. The [[Damage Buff]] will remain on the tower, even if its shield is gone. Unassigning a tower with '''Medic''' will however remove the buffs from it. While this ability is active, the '''Medic''' itself gains the [[Freeze Immunity]].

The '''Medic''' has a placement limit of 4.

==Strategy==
*The '''Medic''' has the ability to permanently grant a [[Firerate Buff]] and temporarily grant a [[Damage Buff]] to towers, making it very useful as a support tower.
**However, unlike other towers like the [[Commander]], [[DJ Booth]] or even the [[Mercenary Base]], it is not able to apply it to every tower in range, it can only apply it to a few of them.
***Because of this, players may need to either place down multiple of them to cover most or all of their towers, or only target the most important of their towers.
***Due to its limited tower selection, towers with naturally low placement limits like the [[Turret]] or [[Gatling Gun]] are a great pick when using the '''Medic''', as their low placement limits mean few '''Medics''' are needed to boost them all.
****With all four '''Medics''' placed down, you can still boost up to 20 towers, so towers like the [[Minigunner]] or [[Swarmer]] can still be paired with it, although you might have to leave some towers unboosted.
*The '''Medic''' can give towers a shield, which stops them from being stunned once.
**This does not stop either debuffs or being frozen. These towers are still affected by these effects, but they will not lose their shield from them.
**When its shield is broken, the targeted tower not only loses its immunity to stuns, but also the [[Firerate Buff]] provided by the '''Medic'''. It does not however, lose any of its ''Ubercharge'' buffs.
***If the shield is regenerated in the middle a stun, the stun will removed. This means the duration of towers assigned to a '''Medic''' cannot be longer than its shield regeneration cooldown.
*The '''Medic''' can provide an additional [[Damage Buff]] to targeted towers, but only temporarily.
**This is best used on harder waves when a tough enemy appears.
**On top of this, it also grants the tower [[Stun Immunity]] and [[Freeze Immunity]], making the tower fully invulnerable to stun and freeze, making it rather useful against enemies with offensive capabilities.
*The '''Medic''' can be used to heal back any lost health and overheal the base health.
**As this effect stacks with each '''Medic''' placed down, having four '''Medics''' placed down will result in the player regenerating 80 health at the start of every wave while also adding an additional overheal of 400.
***This does not just stack with other '''Medics''', but also other towers or mechanics like [[Skills]]. For example, if [[Skills]] are fully upgraded, having one [[Medic]] placed down will allow it to overheal the base health to 250 at Level 5, not just 50 or 200.

==Trivia==
*The '''Medic''' is the second tower that has the ability to restore base health, although it is currently the only one to do so.
**The first tower able to do so was the [[Gladiator]], although it has since lost this ability.
**It is not the only way of healing back base health, as the [[Turkey Leg]] and [[Fruit Cake]] consumables can also do that.
*The item the '''Medic''' carries from level 0-4 resembles the [https://wiki.teamfortress.com/wiki/Medi_Gun Medi Gun] from ''[[wp:Team Fortress 2|Team Fortress 2]]''.
**Originally, the '''Medic''' carried a syringe gun that resembled the [https://wiki.teamfortress.com/wiki/Syringe_Gun Syringe Gun] in ''[[wp:Team Fortress 2|Team Fortress 2]]''.
**The tower also had an old description ("''MEDICCCCC!''") that was a reference to ''[[wp:Team Fortress 2|Team Fortress 2]]''.
*At Level 2, [[w:c:r2da:Vitamins|Vitamins]] from ''R2DA'' can be seen on the '''Medic's''' belt.
*The '''Medic's''' Level 3 upgrade name is a reference to the meme [https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/yeah-this-is-big-brain-time Yeah, This Is Big Brain Time].
*During [[Hardcore Testing BETA]], the '''Medic''' had the same upgrade icons and upgrading prices as the [[Mecha Base]], barring the placement cost.
*Until the [[Overhaul v1.0|Overhaul Update]], the '''Medic's''' Level 5 upgrade was misspelled as ''Goddess or Healing''.
*The '''Medic''' was unable to heal base health in the now removed [[Versus]] mode.
*The '''Medic''' originally had a red cross shoulder patch, which violates [[wp:Geneva Conventions|Geneva Conventions Laws]], as it is being used by the [[wp:International Committee of the Red Cross|ICRC]] and is strictly restricted to be associated with real-world medical aid.
*The '''Medic's''' ''Ubercharge'' ability icon came from ''[https://www.roblox.com/games/183929867/Ashea-Reborn Ashea: Reborn]'', where it was used as an icon for the Heal skill.
*From [[Null Medic]]'s logbook description, the '''Medic''' has scars on itself.
