==Description==
The '''Hacker''' is a hardcore tower that hacks enemies, applying various effects to them. It can be purchased from the store for {{Gem|5,500}} or through a gamepass for {{Robux|1,574}}.

It damages enemies using synapses fired from its equipment, which starts off with a smartphone, then some sort of wrist gadget and in the bottom path, a laptop. These beams target a single enemy, dealing single target damage. Any enemy attacked by the '''Hacker''' will have these effects applied to it:
#First hack is ''Unit Conversion'', unlocked at Level 0. When the enemy is killed, it will be converted into a hologram, with reduced health. This hologram will function similarly to a [[Units|unit]], dealing damage to an enemy upon collision. The converted enemy will ignore defense and [[Tank (Attribute)|Tank]] of the enemy it is colliding with. If the converted enemy has defense, this defense will boost the damage it deals to other enemies. [[Bloated]] does also boost the health of such enemies. [[Boss]] enemies are unable to be converted into holograms.<br/>Enemy health is calculated using their max health and the tower's EV. Health is calculated as:<br/><math>\text{Max Health}^\text{EV}</math>
#Second hack is ''Wire Fraud''. Enemies targeted by the '''Hacker''' will have their rewards on death or damage increased. This is a global boost, meaning everyone in the match receives the added income.
#Third hack is ''Slowness''. This applies a slowness effect to any enemy, as long as it is not a [[Boss]] enemy.

The '''Hacker''' also has an ability, ''Hologram Tower'', which allows it to duplicate a tower. It can duplicate any tower on the map and place it down in any spot. This tower will last for a limited amount of time and disappear once the lifetime ends. The cloned tower will have the hologram modifier on it. The ability has a varying cost based on the total cost of the cloned tower. '''Hacker''' cannot clone other '''Hackers''' or itself. If a cloned tower has a placement limit, then there can only be as many clones of that tower from one player at a time as its placement limit. For example, the [[Gatling Gun]] can only have one clone from a player at a time due to the tower having a placement limit of one.

The '''Hacker''' has hidden detection from Level 2. It has a placement limit of two per player.

==Strategy==
*Duplicate towers with low placement limits, such as the [[Juggernaut]], [[Turret]], [[Necromancer]], [[DJ Booth]] or [[Gatling Gun]] for the most value.
**Do note however that the lifespan of cloned towers are very short. This makes the bottom path ideal for this, as it has the longest clone lifespans.
***Additionally, due to the cooldown being 60 seconds, it is possible to have more than 1 clone. This can be further improved by using the [[Skills|Skill]] Accelerator to reduce the cooldown to 52.5 seconds.
**The [[DJ Booth]] can help by reducing tower costs, making them effectively cheaper to clone.
*The '''Hacker''' will convert any enemy it is targeting into a hologram, dealing collision damage to enemies.
**Top path is preferred for this, as its high DPS and stronger EV allows it to create stronger enemies.
**This is most useful in modes where a large number of high health enemies are present, most preferably [[Polluted Wasteland II]].
**As enemies only need to be targeted by the '''Hacker''' to be converted, it is recommended to have towers with high DPS, such as the [[Accelerator]], damage the enemies in order to convert them faster.
**These enemies will bypass defense and [[Tank (Attribute)|Tank]]. However, they cannot damage [[Flying]] or [[Ghost (Attribute)|Ghost]] enemies.
**If an enemy has defense, that defense will boost how much damage they deal.
***Because of this, do be aware when using defense dropping towers with the '''Hacker'''. While they will make converting enemies faster, they will also reduce the damage dealt.
*While majority of enemies will still perform their on death abilities, some of them will be partially or fully negated. For example, the [[Breaker4]] will only summon one [[Breaker3]] on death when converted instead of three.
**Some enemies can have their second phase cancelled by the '''Hacker'''. For example, the [[Abomination]] will be converted over before it changes into the Reanimated.
*The '''Hacker''' can be used to slow down enemies, making it easier to kill them.
**Bottom path is best for this, as it has a far better slowness than top path.
**Only [[Boss]] enemies are immune to this slowdown.
***Additionally, its slowness can stack with other towers, which means, combined with the [[Toxic Gunner]] and [[Harvester]]'s thorns, they can slow down every non-boss enemy by up to 95%.
*The '''Hacker''' can be used to boost cash gained from enemies, allowing for easier cash generation.
**Bottom path is best for this, as it boosts the cash gained far more than top path.
*To utilize the '''Hacker's''' high potential for dealing with crowds, set each '''Hacker’s''' targeting to ''Strongest'' and ''Weakest'', respectively.
*Setting the '''Hacker''' to ''Weakest'' can ensure that the tower will target and kill enemies on low health.
*When using [[Firework Technician]], each Synapses has their own chance to proc fireworks, resulting in the '''Hacker''' being way more likely to generate one.
**This can be helpful for bottom path '''Hacker''' to make up for its lower DPS.

==Trivia==
*The '''Hacker’s''' Level 4 upgrade name is a reference to the ''[[wp:Intel Core|Intel Core]]'' ''i9'' processor.
**The string of numbers "3260590327" is the Place ID of Tower Defense Simulator on Roblox.
*The '''Hacker''' was first teased all the way back in 2020, but did not make an appearance until the 11th of June 2025 in [[v1.67.0]].
*Bottom path '''Hacker''' has the second worst cost efficiency in the entire game, beaten by [[Swarmer]] at {{Money|5,800}}.
**However, the [[Swarmer]] can reach higher cost efficiency with all its bee stacks applied than the [[Hacker]] with all of its synapses accounted for.
*The '''Hacker''' is the only Hardcore tower at the time of [[Null & Void Event|Null & Void]] to not receive a null enemy variant.
