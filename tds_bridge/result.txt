==Description==
'''v1.30.0''' is the eighty-third update to be released in Tower Defense Simulator, on the 4th of September 2024. It added two skins, the {{Color|Other|Mako}} [[DJ Booth]] and the {{Color|Other|Plushie}} [[Cowboy]].

This update encompassed three versions, the main v1.30.0 update (released 4 September 2024) and two minor updates, the v1.30.1 update (released 4 September 2024) and v1.30.2 (released 11 September 2024).

==Additions==
===Skins===
*{{Color|Other|Mako}} [[DJ Booth]]

===Miscellanous===
*New skin rarity: {{Color|Ultimate|Ultimate}}
**{{Color|Ultimate|Ultimate}} skins can have special interactions with towers, enemies and the environment.
*New [[Codes|code]] obtained by purchasing a [[Cowboy Plush]]
**Code is unique for each [[Cowboy Plush]] bought
**Gives {{Color|Other|Plushie}} [[Cowboy]] skin

==Changes & Bugfixes==
*The [[Engineer]] {{Color|ContentCreator|Wikia}} skin has been put on sale for a day.
*Beating Wave 5 in the [[Tutorial]] now sets the cash to {{Money|1,600}} instead of {{Money|2,200}}.
*Fixed projectile towers dealing damage to units.
*Fixed constantly switching between [[Time Scale]] speeds not progressing the timer, which allowed for a wave to be much longer than intended.
*Fixed [[DJ Booth]]'s cooldown when selecting another track.

:In '''v1.30.1''':
*Fixed {{Color|Other|Mako}} skin being impossible to purchase on mobile.

:In '''v1.30.2''':
*Version number changed from ''v1.30.1'' to ''v1.30.2''.
