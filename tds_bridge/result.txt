==Description==
The '''Ace Pilot''' is an intermediate [[Towers|tower]] that can be bought for {{Coins|1,500}}. Unlike other ground towers, the attacking unit of the '''Ace Pilot''', the plane, is an air unit.

The '''Ace Pilot''' will circle around the runway. At Level 2+, this can be changed to a figure eight. With its ''Toggle Reverse'' ability, it is possible to reverse its flight path. The '''Ace Pilot''' is armed with a pair of machine guns on each wing at the base and will shoot any enemy within its patrol radius. At Levels 2–5, the aircraft will periodically drop a bomb directly where the targeted enemy was last, exploding and damaging any enemies caught in the explosion. The explosion is separated into two spheres. Enemies in the inner sphere will be dealt 100% of the stated damage, while damage in the outer sphere decreases linearly by distance from the edge of the inner sphere to the edge of the outer sphere. Since its bombs deal splash damage, they ignore [[defense]], dealing full damage to the enemies with [[defense]]. These bombs can also damage [[Lead (Attribute)|Lead]] enemies. Due to it being an air-based tower, it is immune to stuns, and because it is not a humanoid tower, it is also immune to debuffs from enemies added before [[v1.9.0]]. Additionally, because it is an air-based tower, it can detect [[Flying]] enemies.

At Level 4, the '''Ace Pilot''' will also grant nearby towers hidden detection with its ''Hidden Assist'', giving towers without hidden detection, like the [[Ranger]], the ability to detect [[Hidden (Attribute)|Hidden]] enemies. Its machine guns are then replaced by a single autocannon, granting it higher damage and firerate. The '''Ace Pilot''' boasts a high DPS, but its limiting range and inconsistent circling cripple its full potential. Therefore, in most situations, it does not operate at the highest DPS most of the time.

The '''Ace Pilot''' has a placement limit of eight per player.

==Strategy==
*Place this tower where it can be used to its full potential. This primarily involves placing it at turns.
**Placing it on a straight path is not recommended, as it will not shoot as often.
**As the '''Ace Pilot''' flies clockwise, placing it so it covers a counterclockwise path turn will mean it has a greater chance of attacking an enemy.
***The first roundabout on [[Wrecked Battlefield]] is a good example of this.
***You can also place it on a clockwise path and use the ''Toggle Reverse'' ability to make it fly counterclockwise, having the same effect.
*When changing flight paths, the '''Ace Pilot''' will complete the previous flight path first before switching to the new flight path.
*The [[DJ Booth]] can significantly increase its attack radius.
**However, the [[DJ Booth]] does not increase the '''Ace Pilot's''' flying radius.
*Try not to focus solely on '''Ace Pilots'''.
**This is because faster enemies may walk right past the '''Ace Pilot''' without being damaged. If you want reliable defense, then a [[Minigunner]] or [[Cowboy]] could be a more reliable option.
*The [[Commander]] cannot decrease the firerate of the bombs. However, it can decrease the firerate of the guns.
*Unlike the [[Pursuit]], the '''Ace Pilot''' does not stop to hover over an area whenever an enemy enters its range.
**This means that the '''Ace Pilot''' is unlikely to be attacking all the time, reducing the effectiveness of the tower.
*In [[Hardcore Mode|Hardcore]] mode, the '''Ace Pilot''' can be used as an effective way to deal with flying, lead and enemies with [[defense]] that appear early on during the game, such as the [[Lead]], [[Hefty]] and [[Balloon]].
*The bombs that the '''Ace Pilot''' drops are a good way to clear out hordes of weaker enemies, like the [[Abnormal]] or [[Slow]], while also dealing additional damage to higher health enemies, like the [[Abnormal Boss]].
**The '''Ace Pilot's''' bombs completely ignore [[defense]], dealing full damage to enemies with defense. Because of this, it can be useful against hordes of enemies with defense that appear early on during the game, like [[Heavy|Heavies]], [[Abnormal Boss]]es or [[Hefty|Hefties]].
**While the bomb is aimed directly at the target, it may miss the enemy as it takes a little while for the bomb to land.
***At Level 5, the missile has a faster projectile speed, so is less likely to miss the enemy's intended target.
*At Level 4+, the '''Ace Pilot''' gives nearby towers hidden detection for towers that do not have it, such as the [[Ranger]].
**Towers are only granted hidden detection if they are within the radius of the runway, which is quite small.
***This radius cannot be increased by the [[DJ Booth]].

==Trivia==
*It is stated by BelowNatural that the '''Ace Pilot''' was based on the [[w:c:bloons:Monkey Ace|Monkey Ace]] from the ''Bloons Tower Defense'' series.
**However, its main method of distributing damage and the fact that it gives hidden detection are similar to those of the [[w:c:roblox-tower-battles:Aviator|Aviator]] from ''Tower Battles''.
*The '''Ace Pilot''' used to share its Level 2 icon with the [[Mortar]]'s Level 4 icon but upside down.
*This is the first tower to able to give other towers hidden detection, with the second being the [[Firework Technician]].
*Strangely, the [[Soul Stealer (Legacy)|Soul Stealer]] and [[Void Reaver (Legacy)|Void Reaver]] can still spawn a [[Soul]] from the '''Ace Pilot''', despite not being a humanoid tower.
*On Level 2 and 4, the '''Ace Pilot''' upgrade names have capitalization errors.
**Additionally, there are two spaces between "''Aerial''" and "''Ace''" in the Level 3 upgrade name instead of one.
*The Level 5 upgrade name, "''The Surging Sky''" is a reference to a fake leak of the Level 6 '''Ace Pilot''' upgrade.
*The Level 3 upgrade name, "''Aerial  Ace''" shares its name with the {{Color|Basic|Aerial Ace}} skin.
**Both the upgrade name and the skin were also added in the same update. Prior to the change, the Level 3 upgrade was simply just "''Better plane''".
