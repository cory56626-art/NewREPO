==Description==
The '''Golden Minigunner''' is a golden perk for the [[Minigunner]], obtained from the [[Golden Crate]] for {{Coins|50,000}} if you own the [[Minigunner]]. It can also be bought in the [[Daily Store]] for {{Coins|75,000}}.

Before the '''Golden Minigunner''' starts firing, it needs to rev up its minigun. If there are no enemies in its range while revved-up, it will wait for around a second for an enemy to enter its range. If no enemy enters its range in that time, it begins to deactivate the gun and will need to rev the gun up again when an enemy enters its range. It fires bullets at enemies at a fast firerate. The '''Golden Minigunner''' has high DPS and a relatively cheap cost compared to other high DPS towers, like the [[Accelerator]] or [[Engineer]]. 

The '''Golden Minigunner''' has more damage and costs more than its normal counterpart, the [[Minigunner]], making it a direct upgrade in many ways. It is mostly considered a mid-game to late-game tower, although it can be used in early game if you have enough base health and focus solely on using the [[Farm]] for some time.

==Strategy==
*It is highly recommend to get 2 Level 3 '''Golden Minigunners''' as both of them could deal 260 total DPS while being more cost efficient compared to a single Level 4.
*The '''Golden Minigunner''' should be one of the towers that are placed down the most. If possible, place them in a large group near the front of the track, or place them in an area that covers a lot of path (for example, inside corners).
**Having a [[Commander]] or [[DJ Booth]] nearby helps a lot.
*Midway through the game, several Level 1-2 '''Golden Minigunners''' are effective in wiping out hordes of enemies for an effective price.
*While this is a cost-effective way to triumph maps, this is not the best tower in terms of DPS.
**In terms of DPS and range, the [[Engineer]], [[Ranger]] and [[Accelerator]] are far more effective. However, they are more expensive and have their own limitations, like placement limits.
***While it does have a quite higher DPS than the normal [[Minigunner]], it also costs more to upgrade.
*This tower is not affected much by the defense from enemies like the [[Giant Boss]] and [[Warden (Modern Enemy)|Warden]] due to its low damage at lower levels. In most cases, the '''Golden Minigunner''' can still deal most of its damage to such enemies, or even its full damage.
**Do note that the Level 3 has 13 damage and Level 4 has 20 damage, which is high enough for most enemies to be able to decrease its damage.
**Even still, defense dropping towers like the [[Pyromancer]] are recommended to increase the damage of '''Golden Minigunners''' against certain enemies with very high defense.
*The '''Golden Minigunner''' can be effective at killing high health hidden enemies that appear in mid game or late game, such as the [[Possessed Armor]] or [[Soul]].
*While the '''Golden Minigunner''' does not have incredibly low range, it is still lower than most other DPS towers, such as the [[Ranger]], [[Engineer]], [[Accelerator]] or [[Turret]].
*If you are planning to use the '''Golden Minigunner''' for early game, you need to have a high amount of base health and farm correctly to afford it. However, this is generally not recommended as it is risky.
**Alternatively, you should have teammates with cheap towers to help deal with the early game while you farm.
***You can also use the [[Supply Drop]] to help you farm more efficiently if you really want to get the '''Golden Minigunner''' down early.

==Trivia==
*The '''Golden Minigunner''', alongside its normal counterpart, the [[Minigunner]], currently has the most skins out of any tower in the game.
*The '''Golden Minigunner''' used to be the most expensive skin in the game, until the [[Coin Crate]]'s addition in [[V1.59.0]], which possesses skin that cost {{Coins|1,346,914,794}}.
