==Description==
The '''Kingpin''', internally referred to as '''EvolvedKingpin''', is the [[Tower Evolution|evolved]] version of the [[Crook Boss]], unlocked by reaching Level 20 on the [[Crook Boss]] tower, either by getting the experience or by skipping the need to get it by paying {{Robux|599}}. Once done, it can be bought for {{Gem|5,500}} and {{Coins|15,000}}. As it is an evolved tower, only some of its upgrades are unlocked at the start. All other have to be unlocked by reaching higher [[Tower Evolution|evolution levels]] on the tower.

The '''Kingpin''' itself is a single target tower with no major gimmicks in the beginning, even lacking the [[Crook Boss]]' summon animation for each unit. It does however have up to three unit queues, all of which summon in can summon a different unit. There are four available options:
*[[Lackey]], a simple single target unit with with decent health and DPS. The unit has hidden detection. It is unlocked from Level 0.
*[[Money Runner]], the only unit that cannot be used offensively outside of collision damage that upon death generates a set amount of cash. It is unlocked from Level 0.
*[[Bouncer]] is a high health melee unit that attacks enemies with a bat. The unit has both hidden and lead detections. It is unlocked from Level 4B.
*[[Contractor]] is a high range unit that deals a lot of damage at a slow speed. It has all three detections. It is unlocked from Level 6B.

While the bottom path has two units unique to itself, the top path instead gains the ''Bounty'' ability which lets the '''Kingpin''' mark an enemy in its range. While doing so, it will stop attacking enemies for 2 seconds. This marked enemy will generate a specific percentage of the enemy's total cash reward once the enemy has been killed, though the reward cannot exceed a specific value. Both the reward percentage and the reward cap increase with upgrades.

The '''Kingpin''' is primarily a mid to late-game tower, with top path focusing on income while bottom path focuses on DPS. It has a placement limit of 4.

==Strategy==
*The '''Kingpin''' is best used as a mid-to-late game tower. 
*At Level 3, the '''Kingpin''' gains two paths.
**The top path is focused on giving the player [[cash]] through its ''Bounty'' ability and upgrading the [[Money Runner]]s it spawns.
***The top path can be used as a secondary income source which may help at affording certain expensive towers like the [[Juggernaut]].
***It is advised to use the ''Bounty'' ability on enemies that have a high cash reward, which usually is enemies with high health.
***Notably it also gains lead detection earlier than its bottom path counterpart.
**The bottom path is more focused on DPS and spawning units, this path unlocks the [[Bouncer]] and the [[Contractor]].
***This path focuses on increasing the efficiency in which the units deal damage, whether it be by increasing their DPS, or shortening their spawnrate.
***Setting the bottom path ''Kingpin'' to spawn either [[Lackey]]s or [[Contractor]]s can be extremely effective on certain maps where they can stack up in a single spot and attack simultaneously.

==Trivia==
*The '''Kingpin''' is one of the first three evolved towers revealed in 2020 prior to the release of the [[Overhaul v1.0|Overhaul Update]].
*The '''Kingpin's''' top path upgrade names are likely referencing those of the [[Golden Crook Boss]].
*The '''Kingpin's''' voice actor is [https://x.com/mitchmarcmedia Mitch Kaye].
*Kingpins are usually drug lords, in real life.
