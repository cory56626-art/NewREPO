==Description==
The '''Turret''' is an advanced [[Towers|tower]] with long range and high damage. It cannot be purchased from the store, instead unlocked at {{Level|50}} or via its [[Gamepasses|gamepass]], priced at {{Robux|340}}. When the player reaches {{Level|50}}, the '''Turret''' can be claimed using the pop up message that appears when joining the lobby.

The '''Turret''' excels at single target damage, boasting a strong mix of high range, damage and firerate. However, it is also very expensive. It costs {{Money|7,750}} to place it down, the second most expensive starting cost for any tower. It takes a cumulative total of {{Money|149,000}} to reach the max level, making it the third most expensive tower to fully upgrade in the entire game. In [[PVP]] mode, it does have reduced placement and upgrade costs, but it is still one of the most expensive towers to fully upgrade there. As the '''Turret''' is not a humanoid tower, it is immune to debuffs which are applied by enemies added before the [[v1.9.0]] update. Debuffs from enemies like the [[Boomer]] can still affect it. This however does not make it immune to stuns. Additionally, it has a larger placement footprint than most other towers. It can detect hidden enemies and has lead detection.

Because of its extremely high base cost and very expensive upgrade prices, as well as high DPS, the '''Turret''' is considered a mid-late game tower.

The '''Turret''' has a placement limit of three per player. This is increased to five in [[PVP]] mode.

==Strategy==
*The '''Turret''' has one of the most expensive starting costs for a tower, so it is impractical for the early-game.
**For the early-game, using cheaper towers is recommended.
**Using the [[Farm]] or [[Cowboy]] will allow you to place a '''Turret''' earlier, though, usually this is not recommended as there are better options than this.
*The '''Turret''' has hidden detection by default and lead detection at Level 1, making it useful against enemies with these properties that may appear in the mid and late-game, especially in [[Hardcore Mode|Hardcore]] mode.
*The '''Turret''' has a larger placement footprint than other towers, so be aware of the space that it takes up.
*It is highly recommended to pair the '''Turret''' with the [[DJ Booth]] to decrease the price of its extremely expensive upgrades.
**It can also be helpful at increasing its already large range.
*The '''Turret''' is immune to debuffs applied by legacy enemies. This includes the [[Soul Stealer (Legacy)|Soul Stealer]]'s [[Fatigue Debuff]] or the [[Gunslinger]]'s [[Scared Debuff]]. However, this does not include the [[Boomer]] or [[Necrotic Skeleton]]'s debuffs as they do not apply an icon to the tower.
**The [[Soul Stealer (Legacy)|Soul Stealer]] also cannot spawn a [[Soul]] from it, as the [[Fatigue Debuff]] is never applied.
*While the '''Turret''' does have a very high DPS, one of the highest in the entire game, it also has one of the lowest placement limits.
**Due to this, towers without limits like the [[Minigunner]], [[Golden Minigunner]], [[Swarmer]] or [[Golden Soldier]] can, if enough are placed down, deal more DPS than it.
**This is far less of an issue the more players there are in the match, as with player count the placement limit per player gets decreased, making towers with no limits far less viable while low placement limit towers like the '''Turret''' are completely unaffected.

==Trivia==
*Strangely, the [[Void Reaver (Legacy)|Void Reaver]] can still spawn a [[Soul]] from the '''Turret''', which is not a humanoid tower unless the {{Color|Premium|XR500}} skin is equipped.
**The [[Soul Stealer (Legacy)|Soul Stealer]] cannot spawn a soul from the '''Turret''' unless it is the {{Color|Premium|XR500}} '''Turret'''. The [[Soul Stealer (Legacy)|Soul Stealer]] and legacy [[Fallen Soul (Legacy)|Fallen Soul]] also can only increase the firerate of the {{Color|Premium|XR500}} '''Turret''', but not any other skins.
*The '''Turret''' has a unique placement and upgrade sound.
