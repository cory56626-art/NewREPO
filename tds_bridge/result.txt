<div class="align-center"><span class="mainheader">'''Daily Store'''</span>
----

The '''Daily Store''' is a place where you can purchase crates, skins, emotes and nametags with {{Coins|Coins}} or {{Robux|Robux}}. The items that you can purchase there change every day at [[wp:Coordinated Universal Time|00:00 UTC]]. The items you can purchase from it are the same for every player.
</div>

{{RightTOC}}

==Daily Item Store==
This section features five randomly selected items from any category. These can be any category as long as it refreshes every day.

==Daily Skin Store==
In the '''Daily Skin Store''', fifteen random skins will appear. These are for the most parts skins from on sale crates which have costs associated to either rarities or specific skins. Additionally, certain skins not obtainable from crates, like the [[Star Wars Skins]], [[Content Creator Skins]] and certain {{Color|Ultimate}} skins can appear here too.

It is possible to purchase skins for towers the player does not own. Additionally, the store comes with a skin preview, allowing you to see how the skin look before buying it.

This section went through various versions.
*Prior to the [[v1.6.0|Violent Night Update]], only two skins appeared. There would always be one [[Premium Crate]] skin and one [[Basic Crate]] skin. The skins that appeared in the store would also be different for each player, but due to a bug, the skins would not change after a day had passed. Only skins for towers that you owned would appear. It was possible to purchase a skin you already owned, though this would do nothing and only take away your currency.
*Prior to [[v2.7.0]], three skins and later for players affected by gambling restrictions, five skins would would appear every day. These were selected from currently on sale crates which have set costs for specific rarities or skins.

==Daily Emote Store==
In the '''Daily Emote Store''', fifteen random emotes will appear. The emotes that appear in this store only cost {{Coins|Coins}}. The cost of each skin can be found on the [[Emotes]] page in the ''Coins'' section.

All the emotes that appear in the '''Daily Emote Store''', with the exception of [[Garry's Dance]], cannot be obtained in any other way, meaning that you need to wait until an emote appears in the '''Daily Emote Store''' to obtain it. As new players obtain [[Garry's Dance]] for free, it is the only store emote that can be obtained without using the '''Daily Emote Store'''. However, as [[Garry's Dance]] is only given out for free to players that first joined after the [[v1.6.0|Violent Night Update]], players that joined before this update still need to use this store to obtain it.

==Daily Sticker Store==
In the '''Daily Sticker Store''', fifteen random stickers will appear, though as there are only twelve which can appear here, this means the store is the same every day. The stickers that appear in this store only cost {{Coins|Coins}}.

All the stickers that appear in the '''Daily Sticker Store''', with the exception of [[GG]] and [[Flex (Sticker)|Flex]], cannot be obtained in any other way, meaning that you need to wait until an sticker appears in the '''Daily Sticker Store''' to obtain it. As new players obtain [[GG]] and [[Flex (Sticker)|Flex]] for free, they are the only store stickers that can be obtained without using the '''Daily Sticker Store'''.

==Daily Nametag Store==
In the '''Daily Nametag Store''', fifteen random nametags will appear. There will always be two nametags for each rarity. The nametags that appear in this store only cost {{Coins|Coins}}. The cost of each nametag depends on its rarity. {{Color|Legendary}} nametags cost {{Coins|5,000}}, {{Color|Rare}} nametags cost {{Coins|2,000}}, {{Color|Uncommon}} nametags cost {{Coins|1,000}} and {{Color|Common}} nametags cost {{Coins|500}}.

==Daily Crate Store==
In the '''Daily Crate Store''', four random crates would appear. The crates that appeared in this store were the [[Basic Crate]], [[Toy Crate]], [[Cold Front Crate]], [[Ducky Crate]], [[Vigilante Crate]], [[Pirate Crate]], [[Lovely Crate]], [[Jolly Crate]], [[Halloween Crate]], [[Lunar Crate]], [[Premium Crate]], [[Deluxe Crate]] and the [[Golden Crate]], which costs {{Coins|50,000}}. However, the crate had to be on sale for them to appear. The skins from these crates could also appear in the '''Daily Skin Store'''.

The '''Daily Crate Store''' was not the only way to purchase these crates. They could also be bought in the inventory, even if they were not currently in the '''Daily Crate Store'''.

Due to underage gambling laws, players were unable to use the '''Daily Crate Store''' if they were located in the Netherlands, Australia, or Belgium. Additionally, players in the United Kingdom or Brazil who could not verify their age to be over 18 are also restricted from seeing this section. However, in exchange, they would be shown two more skins than normal players.

This section ended up getting replaced by a regular crate store which would show every currently on sale crate, as long as the player does not own every skin from it.
