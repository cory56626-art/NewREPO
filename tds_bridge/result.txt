<div class="align-center"><span class="mainheader">'''Skills'''</span>
----
'''Skills''' are special attributes applied to matches that can affect gameplay. There are four types of '''Skills''': Offensive, Economy, Strategy and Defense. They are unlocked at {{Level|15}}.
</div>

==Mechanics==
<gallery type="slideshow" widths="200" position="right">

'''Skills''' will apply buffs to the player's towers and units. These start off small and linearly become slightly higher. Each '''Skill''' has its own level cap, some being as low as 10 and others having up to 40 levels. In the majority of cases, they only apply to the player who has the '''Skill''' and not every single player in the match. However, if the thing that the '''Skill''' affects is shared by the players, it will function in various ways, like including '''Skill''' boosts from every player or only picking the one with the highest Level.

Each '''Skill''' will start out very cheap, but it will quickly become far more expensive as the equation for the cost is exponential. It is calculated as:<br/>
<math>\text{(Base Cost} \times \text{Level)}^\text{Exponential Value}</math><br/>
The result is first rounded down then rounded to the nearest multiple of 5.

The total cost to fully upgrade every '''Skill''' is {{Coins|824,070}}. To have every single '''Skill''' available to upgrade, the player has to purchase every single '''Skill''' except Precision, Scavenger, Scholar, Re-enforcements and Beefed Up Minions, and level them up to Level 10, which would have a total cost of {{Coins|48,195}}. The lowest amount of currency required to have every '''Skill''' for a specific path unlocked will be listed separately in each '''Skill''' section.

After the player unlocks a '''Skill''' from a specific path, a modifier for that path will appear, applying the effects for the player. The description of the modifier will list every single '''Skill''' owned and how much it boosts specific statistics.

In the [[Settings]], it is possible to disable '''Skills''' with the [[Settings#Skills Enabled|Skills Enabled]] setting.

[[Hidden Wave]] cannot be activated with skills enabled. Certain modes, like [[Voidcore Mode|Voidcore]] or [[PVP]], also disable '''Skills'''.

===Resetting Skills===
If the player is unsatisfied with what '''Skills''' they spent their currency on, they have the option to reset their '''Skills'''. While this will not bring back their {{Coins|Coins}}, it will give them back in the form of {{SkillCredit|}}. These are a unique currency only usable for '''Skills'''. The player is only given back 95% of what they spent, and they have to pay {{Coins|200}} to reset '''Skills'''. Each reset will become more expensive by {{Coins|200}}.

After resetting, '''Skills''' are unable to be upgraded using {{Coins|Coins}}, they are restricted to only {{SkillCredit|}}. Once the player has run out of possible upgrades, the method of upgrading '''Skills''' is {{Coins|Coins}} again.
