==Description==
The '''Necromancer''' is a hardcore spawner [[Towers|tower]] that can be unlocked in the store for {{Gem|2,250}} and has a level requirement of {{Level|50}}. It could have also been obtained through a gamepass for {{Robux|1,500}}.

The '''Necromancer''' fires magic bolts at enemies. At Level 4, these are replaced by beams fired from its book, which are not projectiles. This makes it now lock on enemies, meaning it will not change what enemy it is targeting until the current enemy moves out of range or is killed. These beams deal damage based on the number of enemies targeted. For example, if it only targets one enemy, it will deal 30 damage, 45 damage when targeting two enemies, and 90 damage when targeting three enemies. The '''Necromancer''' also has a soul meter of 105, which it fills up by damaging enemies. When the soul meter is full, the '''Necromancer''' will summon gravestones. The '''Necromancer''' can destroy these gravestones using its ''Raise the Dead'' ability. The '''Necromancer''' will summon gravestones until it reaches the limit at that level. At Level 3, the '''Necromancer''' can now upgrade gravestones. After dealing enough damage it will upgrade the gravestones, regardless of whether they are currently placed down or not. Upgraded gravestones summon stronger units. At Level 4, these gravestones can also deal damage when destroyed. This is done through an explosion which has damage fall off, though it can only hit up to three enemies per explosion.

The units spawned depend on the Level of the tower and the gravestone. At Level 0, Level 1 gravestones can only spawn [[Skeleton (Unit)|Skeletons]], at Level 1, Level 1 gravestones gain the ability to summon [[Sword Skeleton]]s. At Level 2, Level 1 gravestones gain the ability to summon [[Giant Skeleton (Unit)|Giant Skeletons]]. At Level 3, gravestones can be upgraded to Level 2, where they spawn [[Skeleton (Unit)|Skeletons]], [[Skeleton Knight]]s, [[Hallow Guard (Unit)|Hallow Guards]] and [[Giant Skeleton (Unit)|Giant Skeletons]]. At Level 4, gravestones can be upgraded to Level 3, where they spawn [[Skeleton Knight]]s, [[Hallow Guard (Unit)|Hallow Guards]] and [[Executioner Skeleton]]s. The units that spawn from gravestones are random, with each unit having a chance of being summoned, though once the gravestones are at their final Level and they are all placed down, the summoned units will always be the same, based on the limits set for them at a specific '''Necromancer''' level.

The '''Necromancer''' has a placement limit of three per player.

==Strategy==
*Placement is important for the '''Necromancer''', because of the way it spawns gravestones.
**The '''Necromancer''' needs to be placed next to the path so that it can summon gravestones, so you might not be able to utilize the most of its range.
***It is best to bring the '''Necromancer''' on maps with long paths, as it can often cover the entire map while placed at the back. This allows the '''Necromancer''' to stack its units more easily and fill its '''Soul Meter''' more quickly, as it will be continuously attacking at all times with full map range. Maps like [[Honey Valley]], [[Polluted Wasteland II]], and more, allow the '''Necromancer''' to fully utilize the potential DPS of its units, and itself.
*The '''Necromancer''' stops attacking while summoning gravestones, making it unable to attack for a while.
**This does not happen when the '''Necromancer''' upgrades gravestones.
**This makes it unable to defend by itself, as hordes of fast enemies like the [[Bolt]] or [[Fallen]] could get out of its range fairly easily. Thus, bringing in reliable towers like the [[Minigunner]] will help avoid this issue.
*The '''Necromancer's''' ''Raise the Dead'' ability can be used to summon various skeletons, which get quite powerful at later levels.
**It is recommended to wait until gravestones are at their higher level so that it spawns its strongest units.
**The units that spawn have determined limits, meaning that once the maximum amount of gravestones are upgraded to their final level, the summoned units will be the same every single time. If the gravestones are not fully upgraded, the summons will still be random.
**At Level 4, the Level 3 gravestones explode when you activate the ability, which can make them useful as traps to take down groups of weaker enemies and chip the health down of stronger ones. 
*Units spawned by the '''Necromancer''', especially at higher levels, have high health, making them useful against crowds of enemies.
**The [[Executioner Skeleton]]'s axe also deals piercing damage, rapidly dealing damage to any enemy caught by it, though its high firerate and the far distance that its axe travels make it quite inconsistent.
*The '''Necromancer''' has flying detection at Level 0, which can make it fairly useful against flying enemies, and while it does not have hidden detection until Level 2, some of its units can detect [[Hidden (Attribute)|Hidden]] enemies and all of them can deal damage to them, making it still useful against [[Hidden (Attribute)|Hidden]] enemies before being upgraded to Level 2.
**At Level 4, it also gets lead detection, making it useable against [[Lead (Attribute)|Lead]] enemies.
**You should not solely rely on the '''Necromancer''' for these two traits alone, however; there are better and cheaper alternatives such as [[Ace Pilot]], [[Militant]], and [[Golden Crook Boss]].
**That being said, the '''Necromancer's''' pierce and high damage makes it great against hordes of [[Hidden]]s and [[Balloon]]s, only slightly compounded by its slow firerate. At Level 4, it can do quite well against groups of [[Hidden Boss]]es along with supplementary damage with its ability.
*The '''Necromancer''' is quite expensive to upgrade. Because of this, towers like the [[Farm]] or [[Cowboy]] are recommended to afford them more easily.
**The [[DJ Booth]] can also be helpful at reducing its upgrade costs.
*As its units provide it with a huge increase in DPS, having support towers that make upgrading gravestones faster is recommended with this tower.
*The '''Necromancer''' can pair up well with the [[Mercenary Base]], because its [[Field Medic]]s can heal multiple skeletons. This is particularly useful for the [[Executioner Skeleton]] as its primary attack is ranged.
**Additionally, its [[Damage Buff]] can be useful at boosting the '''Necromancer's''' damage, increasing its DPS and making it build graves faster.
*Try stacking [[Executioner Skeleton]]s on the map. To do this, wait for the '''Necromancer''' to place down its gravestones, and check if most of them have reached their max level. Once most of the gravestones are at max level, use the ''Raise the Dead'' ability. Repeat this process continuously.
**Certain maps, like [[Honey Valley]], are better for stacking, as the [[Executioner Skeleton]]s can be both summoned far away from the entrance while also having enemies in range for most of the time, delaying them from reaching the entrance as much as possible.
**This allows the '''Necromancer''' to reach its full potential DPS by stacking the various units from its gravestones. The units can also deal a lot of damage by ramming into enemies, such as the [[Hallow Guard (Unit)|Hallow Guard]] and [[Executioner Skeleton]].
*Paired with [[Firework Technician]], '''Necromancer''' is able to trigger multiple fireworks across multiple enemies.
**Unfortunately, fireworks damage and burn damage do not contribute to the '''Soul Meter'''.

==Trivia==
*A '''Necromancer''' tower was originally planned to be added for the [[Halloween 2020 Event]], however, it was replaced with the [[Toxic Gunner]] to avoid potential drama due to the tower being similar to the [[w:c:roblox-tower-battles:Graveyard (Tower)|Graveyard]] tower from ''Tower Battles''.
*The '''Necromancer''' was the first non-hardcore tower to be obtainable for {{Gem|Gems}}.
*Originally, the '''Necromancer''' was planned to be only available every Halloween, though during the [[Lunar Overture Event]], it was on sale until 2nd of December 2023.
**However, due to a bug, it returned on 21st of December 2023 and was present in the store since. An update on the 15th of January 2025 made the '''Necromancer''' a hardcore tower, now officially making it permanent rather than a bug.
**Because of this, the '''Necromancer''' was considered an event tower and put in the {{Color|Exclusive}} category.
*The '''Necromancer's''' beam attack at Level 4 shares a visual resemblance to the [[w:c:roblox-tower-battles:Plasma Trooper|Plasma Trooper]]'s attack from ''Tower Battles.''
*The original '''Necromancer''' Level 4 upgrade name is a reference to the [[w:c:marvel:Darkhold|Darkhold]] from the ''Marvel Comics''.
*The '''Necromancer''' was planned to be the final reward for the [[Lunar Overture Event#Lunar Overture Season Pass|Lunar Overture Season Pass]], but was later changed to only be obtainable via purchasing with gems on the shop, as BelowNatural stated it would have been "''too easy''" to obtain it from the Season Pass. 
*The '''Necromancer''' was originally meant to deal splash damage, but this was changed to pierce for unknown reasons.
