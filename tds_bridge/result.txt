==Description==
'''v1.51.0''' is the one hundred and fourth update to be released in Tower Defense Simulator, on the 29th of January 2025. It added both the [[Rocketeer]] rework and the [[Lunar Crate]].

This update contains four updates, the main v1.51.0 (released 29 January 2025) and three minor updates, the v1.51.0a (released 30 January 2025), v1.51.1 (released 31 January 2025) and v1.51.1a (released 1 February 2025).

==Additions==
===Skincrates===
*[[Lunar Crate]]
**Contains three skins:
***{{Color|LunarYear|Lunar}} [[Rocketeer]]
***{{Color|LunarYear|Lunar}} [[Hallow Punk]]
***{{Color|LunarYear|Lunar}} [[Harvester]]

===Nametags===
*[[Lunar Nametag]]

==Changes & Bugfixes==
===Towers===
*[[Rocketeer]] reworked.
**Updated accuracy, now accuracy of projectiles can be altered through upgrades.
**Now can fire out multiple rockets at once. Each rocket targets a different enemy in the spread angle of the  [[Rocketeer]], though if there is less enemies than missiles, multiple missiles will target the same enemy.
**Damage update, now can generate fireworks.
**Statistical Changes:
***'''Level 2'''
****Renamed from ''Direct Hit'' to ''Heavier Payload''.
***'''Level 4'''
****Cost decreased from {{Money|20,000}} to {{Money|18,500}}.
****Damage decreased from +250 to -60.
****Range increased from +3 to +5.
****Explosion Range increased from 4 to 4 > 5.
****Missile Count increased from +0 to +3.
****Renamed from ''Artillery Cannon'' to ''Missile Maelstrom''.
*{{Color|Lunar|Masquerade}} [[Medic]] skin appearance changed.
*[[Harvester]] and its {{Color|LunarYear|Lunar}} skin are now available through a limited time gamepass for {{Robux|799}}.
*[[Hallow Punk]] and its {{Color|LunarYear|Lunar}} skin are now available through a limited time gamepass for {{Robux|399}}.
*[[Archer]] gamepass taken offsale.

:In '''v1.51.1a''':
*[[Harvester]] gamepass taken offsale.
*[[Hallow Punk]] gamepass taken offsale.

===Enemies===
*[[Quick]]'s cash given is now mode dependent.
**{{Money|8}} on death in [[Molten Mode|Molten]] mode and [[Fallen Mode|Fallen]] mode.
**{{Money|10}} on death in [[Pizza Party]].
**{{Money|5}} on death if no value is set for the mode.
*[[Scout (Pizza Party)|Scout]] cash given increased from {{Money|400}} to {{Money|500}} upon death.
*[[Breaker2]] cash given is now mode dependent.
**{{Money|25}} on death in [[Easy Mode|Easy]] mode, [[Casual Mode|Casual]] mode, [[Intermediate Mode|Intermediate]] mode, [[Molten Mode|Molten]] mode and [[Fallen Mode|Fallen]] mode.
**{{Money|100}} on death in [[Pizza Party]].
**{{Money|5}} on death if no value is set.
*[[Citizen Plush]] cash given increased from {{Money|300}} to {{Money|400}} upon death.
*[[Shotgunner (Pizza Party)|Shotgunner]] changed.
**Health decreased from 2,500 to 2,250.
**Cash given increased from {{Money|1,000}} to {{Money|1,500}} every 50% health lost.
*[[Marionette]] changed.
**Cash given increased from {{Money|250}} to {{Money|325}} on death.
**[[Stun Immunity]] removed.
*[[Clown]] cash given increased from {{Money|1,200}} to {{Money|1,500}} upon death.
*[[Knight]] cash given increased from {{Money|450}} to {{Money|700}} upon death.
*[[Monkey]] cash given increased from {{Money|800}} to {{Money|1,000}} upon death.
*[[Cobalt Guard]] cash given increased from {{Money|1,000}} to {{Money|1,500}} every 25% health lost.
*[[Commander (Pizza Party)|Commander]] cash given increased from {{Money|2,000}} to {{Money|3,000}} every 25% health lost.
*[[Sock Puppet]] changed.
**Cash given increased from {{Money|1,600}} every 180,000 health lost to {{Money|900}} every 50% health lost.
**[[Stun Immunity]] removed.
**[[Freeze Immunity]] removed.
**Fixed reward threshold being set to an insanely high value.
*[[Balloon Kid]] changed.
**Cash given increased from {{Money|1,250}} to {{Money|1,500}} upon death.
**[[Stun Immunity]] removed.
*[[Minigunner (Pizza Party)|Minigunner]] changed.
**Speed decreased from Below Average (2.5) to Slightly Slow (2.25).
**Cash given increased from {{Money|3,000}} to {{Money|4,000}} every 10% health.
*[[Mimic Umbra]] cash given increased from {{Money|4,000}} to {{Money|6,250}} every 25% health lost.
*[[Voodoo Doll]] changed.
**Cash given increased from {{Money|1,625}} to {{Money|2,000}} every 25% health lost.
**[[Stun Immunity]] removed.
*[[Executioner (Pizza Party)|Executioner Plush]] changed.
**Health decreased from 45,000 to 20,000.
**[[Shield Health]] increased from 55,000 to 65,000.
**Speed decreased from Slightly Slow (2.25) to Slow (1.75).
**Cash given increased from {{Money|900}} to {{Money|1,000}} every 1% health lost.
**[[Stun Immunity]] removed.
*[[Reaper]] changed.
**Defense removed.
**Cash given increased from {{Money|2,500}} every 25% health to {{Money|4,000}} every 10% health.
*[[Wox the Fox]] changed.
**Speed decreased from Very Slow (1) to Extremely Slow (0.9).
**Cash given increased from {{Money|4,500}} to {{Money|7,500}} every 1% health lost.
