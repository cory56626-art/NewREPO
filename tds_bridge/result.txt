==Description==
'''v1.52.0''' is the one hundred and fifth update to be released in Tower Defense Simulator, on the 5th of February 2025. The update contained the [[Badlands II]] mini-rework, as well as a new {{Color|Other|Game Master}} skin for the [[Crook Boss]], based on the popular show [[wp:Squid Game|Squid Game]]. 

This update contains seven updates, the main v1.51.0 (released 5 February 2025) and six minor updates, v1.51.1 (released 5 February 2025), v1.52.2 (released 5 February 2025), v1.52.3 (released 5 February 2025), v1.52.4 (released 5 February 2025), v1.52.4a (released 5 February 2025) and v1.52.4b (released 12 February 2025).

==Changes & Bugfixes==
===Towers===
*A single [[Swarmer]] is now able to stack its stinging effect on an enemy.
*Removed damage drop-off from the [[Rocketeer]], though this does not work due to bugs.
*{{Color|Premium|Fortress}} [[Rocketeer]] skin is now guaranteed to appear in the [[Daily Store]] for an unknown amount of time.
*{{Color|Other|Mako}} [[DJ Booth]] and {{Color|Other|Game Master}} [[Crook Boss]] can now be gifted to other players.
*[[Engineer]] can now be gifted to other players.
*Fixed [[Rocketeer]] missiles being able to target [[Flying]] enemies while there were other non-[[Flying]] enemies nearby while the [[Rocketeer]] had no flying detection.
*Partially fixed [[Swarmer]] attacking enemies even when they are being stung. Now it will prioritize enemies that are not being stung, however, it will continue to attack enemies that are being stung once there are no other enemies in its range.
*{{Color|Toy|Toy}} [[Rocketeer]] appearance changed.
*{{Color|Lunar|Masquerade}} [[DJ Booth]] appearance changed.
*Adjusted {{Color|LunarYear|Lunar}} [[Hallow Punk]]'s weapon grip.
*Adjusted {{Color|LunarYear|Lunar}} [[Harvester]]'s snake body to be more consistent with its animations. 
*Fixed {{Color|Christmas2019|Xmas}} [[Rocketeer]] rolled up green mat not being properly wielded onto the backpack.
*Fixed {{Color|Premium|Steampunk}} [[Rocketeer]] not having all of its hat upgrades.
*Fixed {{Color|LunarYear|Lunar}} [[Rocketeer]] not having all of its shoulder ornaments.
*Fixed {{Color|LunarYear|Lunar}} [[Harvester]] clipping issues.
*Fixed {{Color|Other|Community}} [[Minigunner]] clipping issues, as previously green parts could be seen on its legs.
*Fixed {{Color|Bunny|Bunny}} [[Minigunner]] clipping issues, as previously green parts could be seen around its torso.
*Fixed {{Color|FallenMission|Fallen}} [[Sledger]] left glove having a broken mesh.
*Fixed {{Color|FallenMission|Fallen}} [[Scout]] hood using a broken mesh.
*Fixed {{Color|Krampus|Cryptic}} [[Freezer]] not properly upgrading at Level 4.
*Fixed {{Color|Vigilante|Vigilante}} [[Mortar]] Level 3-5 weapons having an incorrectly positioned cannon.
*Fixed ''Underworld Warden'' [[Mission Quests|Mission Quest]] not tracking [[Pizza Party]] triumphs correctly.
*Fixed {{Color|Pirate|Pirate}} [[Mortar]] appearance not changing properly at Level 5.
*Fixed {{Color|Other|Mako}} [[DJ Booth]] speakers being incorrectly rotated at Level 5.
*Fixed {{Color|ContentCreator|Eggrypted}} [[Commander]]'s weapon slide falling into the void after being placed down.
*Fixed {{Color|OperationICE|Frost Legion}} [[Grenadier]] and {{Color|OperationICE|Frost Legion}} [[Field Medic]] having a large grey cube appear in front of them when seen in the inventory preview.
