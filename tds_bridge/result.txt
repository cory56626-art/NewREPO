==Description==
The '''Pursuit''' is an advanced tower that can be bought for {{Coins|15,000}} after reaching {{Level|100}} or bought via a gamepass for {{Robux|1,500}}. The '''Pursuit''' patrols a specified area, attacking any enemy that enters it that it can also see in its own range. The area it patrols can be changed with its ''Patrol'' ability. It also has two upgrade paths.

Once an enemy enters its range, it fires upon the enemy that fits its targeting, hovering in the air while it fires at the targeted enemy. It only stops firing when all enemies are killed, or all enemies have left its attacking area. It then resumes patrolling its range. It has a limited amount of ammo which, when it runs out, makes it reload.

At Level 3, the '''Pursuit''' gains the ability to fire missiles at enemies, which deal splash damage. The explosion is separated into two spheres. Enemies in the inner sphere will be dealt 100% of the stated damage, while the damage in the outer sphere decreases linearly. Additionally, the '''Pursuit's''' missiles can damage [[Lead (Attribute)|Lead]] enemies. The '''Pursuit's''' can also detect [[Flying]] enemies because it is an air-based tower. If upgraded to the bottom Level 4, it also can detect [[Hidden (Attribute)|Hidden]] enemies.

The '''Pursuit''' is considered to be a mid to late-game tower due to its high DPS and splash damage capabilities, as well as its high cost. 

The '''Pursuit''' has a placement limit of six per player. This is decreased to five in [[PVP]] mode.

==Strategy==
*As the '''Pursuit''' is an air-based tower, it is immune to all stuns. This makes it useful against enemies that can stun towers, like [[Molten Executioner]]s, [[Fallen Honor Guard]]s, [[Unstable Ice]]s and bosses.
**As it is also not a humanoid tower, it is also immune to any debuffs from enemies prior to [[Lunar Overture Event]], that being enemies like the [[Nuclear Monster]] or [[Soul Stealer (Legacy)|Soul Stealer]].
*At Level 3, the '''Pursuit''' gains missiles. These can be quite powerful, as they allow it to deal damage to multiple enemies at once.
**If you want to use the '''Pursuit's''' missiles for their area of effect capabilities, the bottom path upgrade is recommended as it focuses on missiles.
**Due to being explosives, they can bypass the defense of enemies such as [[Frost Hunter]]s or [[Fallen Shield]]s, and many other enemies with defense.
**While the top path's missiles are somewhat inconsistent due to their explosion size, the bottom path's explosion radius is far larger, rendering potential misses extremely unlikely.
*The better buffs to apply to the '''Pursuit''' depend on the upgrade path you want to use the most.
**If you are going to use the top upgrade path, [[Firerate Buff]]s from towers like the [[Commander]] are recommended, as its main DPS comes from its guns.
**If you are going to use the bottom upgrade path, [[Damage Buff]]s from towers like the [[DJ Booth]] or [[Mercenary Base]] are recommended. As its main DPS comes from its missiles, which are not affected by [[Firerate Buff]]s, [[Damage Buff]]s are better here, as they can affect both the guns and the missiles.
**Regardless, both are still beneficial and, preferably, both should be applied.
*The missiles of the '''Pursuit''' can damage [[Lead (Attribute)|Lead]] enemies and both its guns and missiles detect [[Flying]] enemies. This, combined with its high DPS, makes it very useful against these enemies later on during the game.
**However, the '''Pursuit''' cannot directly attack [[Hidden (Attribute)|Hidden]] enemies unless it is upgraded to Level 4 of the bottom upgrade path. It can only damage them indirectly if they are caught in its missile explosions otherwise.
**Due to the price of this upgrade, and the price of the '''Pursuit''' as a whole, it is not advised to rely on this for hidden detection early on.
*Do keep in mind that the '''Pursuit''' has an ammo system, which means it will need to reload after it has depleted its ammo bar.
*The '''Pursuit's''' ''Patrol'' ability is very useful, as it gives the '''Pursuit''' effectively full map range and allows it to keep up with enemies that may escape from its range.
**Do note that, compared to the '''Pursuit's''' range, the cooldown on this ability is less than favorable for the top path, so using ''Patrol'' carefully is advised.
**This ability is even more effective in maps with lots of loops like [[Polluted Wasteland II]] and [[Honey Valley]] as the '''Pursuit''' will not need to be constantly moved.
**The '''Pursuit's''' patrol range is slightly bigger than it looks, as it extends to any area that the '''Pursuit''' can reach with its attack range. When using the ''Patrol'' ability, keeping this in mind can increase the amount of enemies '''Pursuit''' can cover.

==Trivia==
*The '''Pursuit''' was teased on the 15th of May 2020 by BelowNatural in [https://twitter.com/BelowNatural/status/1261492298514300929 this tweet].
*The '''Pursuit''' seems to be based on the [[w:c:bloons:Heli Pilot|Heli Pilot]] from the ''Bloons Tower Defense'' series.
*Before its rework on the 27th of November 2024, the placement sound was taken from the {{RLinkC|88885539|Airstrike}} gear from the Roblox catalog.
*Weirdly, the [[Gamepasses|gamepass]] for the '''Pursuit''' used to be called ''Helicopter'' instead of ''Pursuit'' before 15th of April 2023.
*The '''Pursuit''' is the first tower to have two upgrade paths, with previous towers having only one upgrade path.
**However, the [[Hacker]] was the first tower to originally come with two upgrade paths, as the '''Pursuit''' was an older tower with only one upgrade path prior to its rework in [[v1.43.0]].
*The '''Pursuit''' is the second tower to change the position in other maps without being sold, with the first being [[Brawler]].
**However, unlike [[Brawler]] which can free space for other towers after being moved, the Pursuit's originally placed helipads still have a fixed position and cannot free up space until it is sold.
*The '''Pursuit''' was originally free to be claimed once the player reached {{Level|100}}, but due to the '''Pursuit''' rework in [[v1.43.0]], the '''Pursuit''' needs to be bought from the shop for {{Coins|15,000}} once the player is at {{Level|100+}}.
