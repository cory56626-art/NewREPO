==Description==
The '''Boomerang''' is a starter tower bought for {{Coins|300}}. It attacks enemies by throwing a boomerang at them.

The '''Boomerang''' will lock onto an enemy within its range and aim for 1 second before throwing out a boomerang. This boomerang will travel in an arc and pierces through multiple enemies until it either runs out of enemies to hit or reaches the maximum number of enemies it can hit, where it returns back to the '''Boomerang'''. Regardless of how many enemies the '''Boomerang''' hit visually, it will always only damage as many enemies as what its max hits is set to. Although setting the targeting is possible and visually, the '''Boomerang''' will target enemies based on it, once thrown it will instantly snap back and throw it at the first enemy. This boomerang takes time to travel to an enemy. It will not throw its boomerang again until it has returned to it, meaning its actual firerate is capped based on how long it takes for the boomerang to return.

At Level 3, it will gain a second boomerang, effectively doubling its damage. This will also not function the same as the first one, as it will target the last enemy the first boomerang will hit. For example, if there are six enemies in a line, the '''Boomerang''' will throw the first boomerang at the first enemy in the line and the second boomerang at the fifth enemy. They will begin returning to it once the first boomerang hits the fifth enemy in the line and the second boomerang hits the first enemy in the line.

The '''Boomerang''' is primarily an early to mid-game AOE tower which from Level 0 can be used to damage [[Flying]] enemies and from Level 3+ [[Lead (Attribute)|Lead]] enemies. It has a placement limit of 12.

==Strategy==
*This tower is best placed in areas where it can cover the most of the path.
**Unlike the [[Executioner]], it cannot hit an infinite amount of enemies, meaning positioning it specifically to make it hit as many enemies as possible is not necessary.
*This tower can be used against [[Lead]] and [[Flying]] enemies, but it will struggle against single high-health ones such as the [[Frost Mage]] or the [[Elite Lead]].
**It can however perform well against groups of weaker enemies such as the [[Balloon]].
**Additionally, while the '''Boomerang''' cannot target [[Hidden (Attribute)|hidden]] enemies, it can hit them if the boomerang hits it while travelling to another enemy.

==Trivia==
*The store description of the '''Boomerang''' is a reference to the ''Avatar: The Last Airbender'' franchise.
**This part of the description was originally used by the [[Executioner]], though it was moved to this tower once it was released.
*The Level 4 upgrade name is a reference to ''The Lord of the Rings''.
*The '''Boomerang''' may have been based on the [[w:c:bloons:Boomerang Monkey|Boomerang Monkey]] from ''Bloons Tower Defense''.
