==Description==
The '''Fallen Soul''' is an enemy that appears in [[Fallen Mode|Fallen]] and Fallen Arena [[PVP]] modes. It has a decent amount of health with above average speed. As it is a [[Hidden (Attribute)|Hidden]] enemy, only towers with hidden detection can target it.

==Logbook Description==
"''Fallen Souls were the spirits of those who died before the curse took hold. The Queen's sacrifice broke down the wall between life and death, pulling these souls back into the now-corrupted realm. They drift through battlefields, unable to rest or return to the kingdom they once knew. They are nearly invisible — most people only feel a cold breeze as they pass by.''"

==Appearance==
The '''Fallen Soul''' has dark blue skin. It does not have a head, torso or legs, though it does have a brown skull and rib cage. It wears a grey helmet with blue spikes, grey cloth around its waist and has chains wrapped around its arms. It also had multiple blue spikes on its shoulders and grey gloves.

==Strategy==
*Most early game towers gain hidden detection. Such towers are recommended to deal with early '''Fallen Souls'''.
**Single target towers, like the [[Militant]], [[Golden Soldier]], [[Ace Pilot]] or [[Assassin]], can also clear out groups of '''Fallen Souls''', although they will require more cash to successfully eliminate them.
**As '''Fallen Souls''' spawn in hordes, the [[Golden Pyromancer]] is particularly useful, being able to damage multiple enemies at once and having hidden detection by default.
***The [[Pyromancer]] can also be useful against '''Fallen Souls''', although it costs more and does not have hidden detection at base level.
**Though the [[Trapper]] cannot detect hidden enemies, its traps will still work against them. In particular, its landmines can be used to eliminate groups of '''Fallen Souls''' due to its high splash damage.
*For splash damage towers that cannot target '''Fallen Souls''', such as the [[Mortar]], if a '''Fallen Soul''' is caught up in an explosion, they will take damage from the explosion range.
**This means that another detectable enemy must be near the '''Fallen Soul''' for a splash damage tower to hit the '''Fallen Soul'''.
**Similarly, the [[Tesla]] cannot target the '''Fallen Soul''', however, it can hit it if there are other detectable enemies nearby.
