==Description==
The '''Executioner''' is a ranged ground [[tower]] that was unlocked by triumphing [[Totality Tower]]. It could have also been obtained through a gamepass for {{Robux|750}} during the [[Solar Eclipse Event]]. The '''Executioner's''' most recent sale had it require {{Robux|799}} to purchase.

The '''Executioner''' attacks by throwing its axe at an enemy. The axe will travel to that enemy and damage it, and then bounce to other enemies. Any enemy that is in '''Executioner's''' range when the axe is thrown may be targeted. The enemy it bounces onto depends on the '''Executioner's''' current targeting option. This axe can bounce 3 times (4 times at Level 3-4 and 7 times at Level 5). If the axe runs out of bounces or there are no enemies to bounce to, it will travel back to the '''Executioner'''. The '''Executioner's''' axe can also hit other enemies without directly targeting them if they are in its flight path, even if it used all of its bounces. However, it can only hit up to three enemies at once per axe tick. The effective firerate of the '''Executioner''' depends on when the axe returns to it; if the axe bounces multiple times or it travels long distances to its targets there will be a greater delay per attack. 

The '''Executioner's''' high damage and ability to damage multiple enemies makes it a great tower for area of effect. However, it is very weak against singular enemies. The '''Executioner''' has hidden detection at Level 4 and flying detection at Level 0.

The '''Executioner''' has a placement limit of 9 per player.

==Strategy==
*Because of how the '''Executioner''' works, the axe may take longer to return to it, increasing its firerate.
**Because of this, the [[Commander]] will be less effective or have no effect on the '''Executioner's''' firerate if the projectile travel time is longer than the firerate.
*Each hit of the '''Executioner's''' axe can trigger a firework from the [[Firework Technician]].
**Since usually the axe will be hitting multiple times, it will drastically increase the odds of getting a firework by sheer quantity, which will also greatly increase the '''Executioner's''' damage output.
*The targeting option of the '''Executioner''' affects what enemy its axe will bounce onto. Certain targeting options will allow the '''Executioner's''' axe to hit more enemies, increasing its DPS.
**Setting the '''Executioner's''' targeting to ''Farthest'' will make its axe bounce onto enemies the furthest distance away from the '''Executioner''', making it travel on a longer route, which gives it more time to hit enemies.
*Due to the '''Executioner's''' ability to damage multiple enemies, this can be used as a great tower to clear out hordes of weaker enemies and even minor bosses at Level 5.
*The '''Executioner''' may damage an enemy more than once, effectively increasing its DPS.
*The '''Executioner''' should be placed far away from the path, as it makes its axe travel on a longer route, giving it time to attack more enemies.
**The '''Executioner's''' range can be increased by the [[DJ Booth]].
**If the '''Executioner''' is placed next to a path, it may not be able to hit an enemy that is next to it.
*The '''Executioner''' gains hidden detection at Level 4, however because of how expensive it is to upgrade it to that level, it is recommended to bring other towers with cheap hidden detection, if you are planning to use this early on during the game.
**Later on in the game, the '''Executioner''' can deal with hordes of high health hidden enemies, like the [[Soul]] and [[Shadow Boss]].
**While the '''Executioner''' can indirectly damage hidden enemies before Level 4 through bouncing, this is unreliable and unlikely to eliminate many of them.
*The '''Executioner''' can do well against the [[Mystery]], as it can both damage the [[Mystery]] and the enemy it spawned if you are lucky enough.
*The '''Executioner''' can detect flying enemies, making it useful in [[Hardcore Mode|Hardcore mode]] against the [[Balloon]] and in [[Pizza Party]] against [[Balloon Kid]]s.
**However, the '''Executioner''' cannot damage lead enemies, making it useless against the [[Lead Balloon]].
*Towers that can slow down enemies, like the [[Electroshocker]], [[Toxic Gunner]] or [[Sledger]] are very useful with the '''Executioner''', as they give it more time to damage enemies.

==Trivia==
*The '''Executioner''' is the second ground tower that can detect flying enemies, with the first being the [[Hunter]].
*The '''Executioner''' may have based been on the [[w:c:clashroyale:Executioner|Executioner]] card from ''Clash Royale'' and the [[w:c:bloons:Boomerang Thrower|Boomerang Thrower]] from the ''Bloons Tower Defense'' game series.
**The store description of the '''Executioner''' is a reference to the ''Avatar: The Last Airbender'' franchise.
**The '''Executioner's''' flying detection may have also been based on the [[w:c:clashroyale:Executioner|Executioner]] card's ability to attack air troops.
*The '''Executioner''' is planned to be reworked in the third quarter of 2026.
