==Description==
The '''Electroshocker''' is an intermediate tower that has the capability to deal damage through chain reactions. At later levels, it also becomes a support tower as it gains the ability to stun enemies and reduce [[Defense]]. It can be purchased in the store for {{Coins|2,500}}. It can also damage [[Lead (Attribute)|Lead]] enemies from Level 0 and detect [[Hidden (Attribute)|Hidden]] enemies from Level 2 onward.

The '''Electroshocker''' shoots a lightning-chain projectile from its ''Electro Gun'' that arcs toward and damages (where applicable) a set number of enemies, targeting the closest enemy to the original target. The '''Electroshocker''' can also stun enemies for 0.1 seconds at Level 0 which increase as with upgrades up to 0.25 seconds at Level 5. This completely stops the enemy. For enemies with [[Stun Immunity]], the enemy is not stunned but rather slowed down by 40%. [[Boss]]es are not slowed down as much as regular enemies, since the slowdown is 20% on them.

From Level 5, it can melt 20% [[Defense]] with every hit, though it can only remove up to 20% of the defense. For example, if an enemy has 50% defense, the defense can be decreased by up to 10%, down to 40%.

The '''Electroshocker''' is considered an all-rounder tower, as its cheap cost allows it to be used for early-game dps, especially against [[Lead (Attribute)|Lead]] enemies, while its stunning capabilities, [[Defense]] reduction, chain damage and decent DPS, especially from Level 4+, make it extremely effective in the mid to late game.

The '''Electroshocker''' has a placement limit of seven per player.

==Strategy==
*The '''Electroshocker''' can be used to clear out hordes of weaker enemies, though it will do worse against enemies with higher health, such as the [[Fallen Squire]].
*Use the '''Electroshocker''' in areas that enemies pass multiple times or at corners, as it gives the '''Electroshocker''' more opportunities to slow down and damage enemies.
**Intersections such as in the map [[Crossroads]] are also viable options.
*The '''Electroshocker''' can be very useful against fast enemies, such as the [[Fallen Rusher]], while also dealing a decent amount of damage against them, especially when they come in hordes.
*While the maximum range is stated as 17, due to the chain range, this can be further extended.
**Chain range works by shocking an enemy within another enemy's range, so long as they are within the chain range of that enemy. For example, an enemy outside the range of the '''Electroshocker''' may still be shocked if they are within the chain range of another enemy that was shocked. This only applies if the '''Electroshocker''' has not reached its maximum number of hits.
**This can allow the '''Electroshocker''' to indirectly hit undetectable enemies, like [[Flying]] enemies indirectly, such as the [[Balloon]]. Paired with its ability to hit [[Lead (Attribute)|Lead]] enemies, it can deal with [[Lead Balloon]] pretty well, only if other detectable enemies are in range.
**You can use the [[DJ Booth]] to increase the range of the '''Electroshocker'''.
***However, the chain range is not increased.
*Since the '''Electroshocker''' can stun any enemy without [[Stun Immunity]], it can be useful at any point in the game.
**Unlike most other slowing towers, its stun is not affected by size, making it quite good against larger enemies, like the [[Fallen Tank]], or even major bosses, such as the [[Fallen King]].
***For enemies with the  [[Stun Immunity]], there is no stun effect; rather, the enemy is slowed down by up to 40%. For [[Boss]] enemies, that effect is significantly weaker on them, though they are still slowed down by 20%.
*The '''Electroshocker''' pairs well with towers that take time before they start attacking, such as the [[Minigunner]] and [[Accelerator]].
**The '''Electroshocker''' also pairs well with towers with a slow firerate, like the [[Ranger]] and [[Mortar]], as it gives them more time to attack enemies with their slow firerate.
*The '''Electroshocker''' has lead detection, allowing it to deal with lead enemies, like the [[Lead]]. In later Levels a cluster of [[Electroshocker]]s can deal with tankier late-game lead enemies like [[Deep Freeze]].
*You can use the [[Commander]] to decrease the firerate of the '''Electroshocker''', which increases the number of times that it can stun enemies within its range.

==Trivia==
*The vest that the Level 3+ '''Electroshocker''' wears is the [[w:c:r2da:Faraday Vest|Faraday Vest]] from ''R2DA'', which in turn is named after the scientist [[wp:Michael Faraday|Michael Faraday]], who studied electricity.
**The creator of this vest is BelowNatural, the creator of Tower Defense Simulator.
*This tower may be very loosely based on the [[w:c:roblox-tower-battles:Plasma Trooper|Plasma Trooper]] and/or the [[w:c:roblox-tower-battles:Stunner|Stunner]] from ''Tower Battles''.
*The Tesla Tower, originally used by the first versions of the '''Electroshocker''' was a nod to the [[wp:Wardenclyffe Tower|Wardenclyffe Tower]] in Shoreham, Long Island, New York, created by [[Wikipedia:Nikola Tesla|Nikola Tesla]].
*The first Level 4 upgrade name, "MOAR POWER" could have been a reference to ''Bloons Tower Defense 6'' as there is an upgrade for the [[w:c:bloons:Boomerang Monkey (BTD6)|Boomerang Monkey]] called "M.O.A.R. Glaives". 
**However, "M.O.A.R." actually stands for Multiple Object Advanced Ricochet in ''Bloons Tower Defense 6''.
**It could also be a spoof on "more power".
*The Level 5 upgrade name, "Zeus Cannon" is a reference to an upgraded [[w:c:callofduty:Thundergun|Thundergun]] from the ''Call of Duty'' franchise, specifically the Zombies mode.
*The '''Electroshocker''' used to be an advanced tower prior to the [[v1.84.0]] update.
