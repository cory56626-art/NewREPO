==Description==
The '''Juggernaut''' or '''EvolvedJuggernaut''' is the [[Tower Evolution|evolved]] version of the [[Minigunner]], unlocked by reaching Level 20 on the [[Minigunner]] tower, either by getting the experience or by skipping the need to get it by paying {{Robux|599}}. Once done, it can be bought for {{Gem|6,000}} and {{Coins|15,000}}. As it is an evolved tower, only some of its upgrades are unlocked at the start. All other have to be unlocked by reaching higher [[Tower Evolution|evolution levels]] on the tower.

The '''Juggernaut''' starts out working very similarly to the [[Minigunner]]. When enemies enter its range, it will rev up its minigun and start firing at them. It will not differ mechanically until Level 4, where it gains two paths.
#The top path is a more support based path where it gains a secondary range in which it reduces stun, freeze and debuff durations. Any tower in that range will have orange shields around them.
#The bottom path is more offensive based as it gives the passive ability of dealing boosted damage to [[Boss]] enemies.

The '''Juggernaut''' has hidden detection from Level 2, flying detection from Level 4A and lead detection from Level 4B. It has a placement limit of 1.

== Strategy ==
*The '''Juggernaut''', due to its extraordinarily high costs to place and upgrade, is impractical for lower difficulties, such as [[Intermediate Mode|Intermediate]] and [[Casual Mode|Casual]] Modes. Also due to this, it is strongly recommended to bring [[Farm]] or [[Golden Cowboy]] to help afford its extreme prices.
*As it has a placement limit of one and the largest placement footprint in the game (tied with [[DJ Booth]]), you should carefully consider where to place it. 
*If your current gamemode has more [[Flying]] enemies than what your current defence can defeat, and/or a lot of enemies that can apply stuns and/or debuffs to your towers, it is recommended you upgrade the '''Juggernaut''' in its top path. Conversely, should you find multiple high-health bosses and/or many high-health enemies with Lead, you should upgrade '''Juggernaut''' in its bottom path. To capitalise on the Boss Damage muliplier, you can set '''Juggernaut''' to Strongest.
*It is strongly recommended to use the DJ Booth to help reduce the '''Juggernaut'''’s upgrade prices, among other benefits. To maximize its offensive potential, you can shield it with the [[Medic]] tower, boost its firerate with [[Commander]], and increase its damage with either a red track DJ Booth or a level 4+ [[Mercenary Base]].

==Trivia==
*The '''Juggernaut''' was originally intended to release alongside the [[Overhaul v1.0|Overhaul Update]] as an evolved counterpart to the [[Minigunner]], but would be cut for undisclosed reasons, likely due to time constraints.
*The '''Juggernaut''' tower is the most expensive tower in the game's history, regardless of whether the player uses the top or bottom path, costing a total of {{Money|532,000}} to completely max out the tower if on the bottom path, or {{Money|400,200}} otherwise, followed by the [[Gatling Gun]].
**Although, if developer-exclusive towers are counted, the [[Golden Snowballer]] is more expensive overall, costing {{Money|1,002,609}} in total.
*The '''Juggernaut''' is the first tower to cost both {{Coins|Coins}} and {{Gem|Gems}} simultaneously.
*The '''Juggernaut''' is the first tower to have seven upgrades.
*The '''Juggernaut''' is the first tower to evolve from one another—in which this tower is evolved from the [[Minigunner]]—followed by the [[Operator]] which is evolved from the [[Scout]].
*The orange shield seen around towers in '''Juggernaut's''' ''Fortify'' range may be a reference to the [[Shield]] seen on certain enemies prior to the [[Overhaul v1.0|Overhaul Update]].
*The '''Juggernaut's''' quote in Experimental Weaponry is likely a reference to [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EzWNBmjyv7Y Thanos in the end credits scene for the ''Age of Ultron''].
*The '''Juggernaut's''' final upgrades on both paths share names with shark species.
