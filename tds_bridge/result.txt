<div class="align-center"><span class="mainheader">'''Crates'''</span>
----

'''Crates''' in Tower Defense Simulator are items used to for the most part unlock '''Skins''' for [[towers]] and [[consumables]]. '''Crates''' can be bought with {{Coins|Coins}}, {{Robux|Robux}} or event [[currencies]], like {{Shards|Shards}}. Some '''Skincrates''' are event-exclusive, such as the [[Bunny Crate]] and the [[Spooky Crate]]. This means they cannot be bought after the event has ended. Some '''Skincrates''' can be obtained through [[Login Rewards]], [[Index]] or [[Passes]]. The [[Golden Crate]] also unlocks a Golden Perk along the {{Color|Golden|Golden}} skin, which affect multiple parts of the tower, mainly being its stats.

Each '''Skincrate''' has specific skins that generally only they can unlock, with some exceptions. One '''Skincrate''' unlocks one skin and each '''Skincrate''' has a limited amount of skins to unlock. You can buy excess '''Skincrates''', though they cannot be opened.

For '''Consumable Crates''', all of them share all or a portion of their consumables, though it is more likely to get consumables with higher rarity in higher tier crates.

To open a '''Skincrate''', you must go into the ''Crates'' section of your inventory while in the lobby and open the crate in question. Using a '''Skincrate''' will randomly assign a skin that can be obtained from that crate to you. A specific skin for a specific tower cannot be chosen beforehand. You cannot get a skin for a tower that you do not own.

If a player is from the Netherlands, Australia and Belgium, or if a player is in the United Kingdom and Brazil that cannot verify their age to be over 18, you will be able to see the order in which you unlock skins (or items you will get in the next crate) to comply with gambling laws.

<div class="align-center"><span class="mainheader">'''Skins'''</span>
----

'''Skins''' are just cosmetic overhauls of the [[Towers|tower]] in question, with no added bonus. Not all '''Skins''' can be found in '''Skincrates''' as some can only be obtained through different means, like a [[Codes|code]], such as the {{Color|Other|Spring Time}} [[Commander]] or from [[Passes]], such as the [[Duck Hunt Event#Skins|{{Color|Ducky|Ducky}}]] skins. To equip a skin, you must go into the ''Towers'' section of your inventory while in the lobby and select the specific tower you want to equip a skin for.

Every skin has a rarity. For the skincrates that appear in the store, there are four rarities that they can have which determine how likely you are to unlock it. These rarities are {{Color|Common|Common}}, {{Color|Uncommon|Uncommon}}, {{Color|Rare|Rare}} and {{Color|Legendary|Legendary}}. For the [[Golden Crate]], they all use the {{Color|Golden|Golden}} rarity instead, making them all equal. Skins obtained directly from events and some seasons use the {{Color|Event|Event}} rarity, making them all equal. Skins obtained directly via Robux or from [[PVP]] rewards use the {{Color|Ultimate|Ultimate}} rarity. Other skins, such as code skins use the {{Color|Exclusive|Exclusive}} rarity. Skins that are in legacy crates from before 2023 also use the {{Color|Event|Event}} and the {{Color|Exclusive|Exclusive}} rarity. There are a few exceptions where a skin should have a {{Color|Event|Event}} or {{Color|Exclusive|Exclusive}} rarity, but it uses a different rarity instead.
