==Description==
The '''Firerate Buff''' is a buff that decreases a tower's firerate, making them fire faster. It is displayed on the towers using an icon of a lightning bolt.

==Mechanics==
Currently, the '''Firerate Buff''' is calculated as:
::<big>'''Original Firerate ÷ (1 + Firerate Buff)'''</big>
:::where '''Firerate Buff''' is the buff supplied is in decimal form (for example, 10% is 0.1).

While it may look like the firerate of a tower with the '''Firerate Buff''' is rounded up or down to the third decimal, this is only visual. 

Prior to the [[v2.0.0]] update, the firerate of a tower with the [[Firerate Bug]] was calculated as:
::<big>'''(Bugged Firerate - [[Firerate Bug|Firerate Bug Increase]]) ÷ (1 + Firerate Buff) + [[Firerate Bug|Firerate Bug Increase]]'''</big>
:::where ''Bugged Firerate'' was the firerate with the [[Firerate Bug]].

Prior to the [[V1.1.0|Entity System Update]], the '''Firerate Buff''' was calculated as:

::Without the [[Firerate Bug]]:
:::<big>'''Original Firerate × (1-Firerate Buff)'''</big>
::With the [[Firerate Bug]]:
:::<big>'''((Bugged Firerate - [[Firerate Bug|Firerate Bug Increase]]) × (1 - Firerate Buff)) + [[Firerate Bug|Firerate Bug Increase]]'''</big>

The following towers are either partially or fully not affected by the '''Firerate Buff''':
*All [[units]].
*Ability cooldowns.
*Units spawnrates.
*The [[Commander]]; the [[Commander]] cannot boost the firerate of other [[Commander]]s.
*Firerate of secondary attacks, like the [[Pursuit]]'s missiles.
**Their main firerate can still be decreased.
*Tick of the [[Pyromancer]], [[Toxic Gunner]], [[Cryomancer]], [[Slasher]] and [[Swarmer]].
**The tick of the [[Accelerator]] can still be decreased.
*The rev-up of the [[Minigunner]].
*Charge-up and cooldown of the [[Accelerator]].
**The cooldown of burst towers like the [[Soldier]] or [[Toxic Gunner]] also cannot be decreased.
*The [[Executioner]]; the firerate will not be decreased if the axe takes too long to return.
*The [[Archer]]; the '''Firerate Buff''' can decrease the time it takes for it to reload its bow, but it cannot decrease the time it takes it to aim at enemies.

The pump time for the [[Enforcer]] is also affected by this buff.

==Applied By==
The following towers have the '''Firerate Buff''':

*[[Gladiator]] (''Warrior's Call'' ability)

The following towers or consumables can apply the '''Firerate Buff''' to other towers:

*[[Commander]] (Passive and ''Call to Arms'' ability)
*[[Medic]] (Passive to assigned towers)
*[[War Machine]] (Passive)
*[[Cooldown Flag]] (Passive)
*[[Sugar Rush (Consumable)|Sugar Rush]] (Passive)
*[[Easter Egg]] (Passive for 20 seconds)
The following event enemies can apply the '''Firerate Buff''' to towers:

* [[Null Guardian]] (''Null Aura'' ability, [[Null & Void Event|Null & Void]])
* [[Null Guardian Clone]] (''Null Aura'' ability, [[Null & Void Event|Null & Void]])
The following event features can apply the '''Firerate Buff''' to towers:

* [[The Chariot]] (Passive, [[Null & Void Event|Null & Void]])
