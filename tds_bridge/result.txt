==Description==
The '''Mortar''' is an advanced cliff [[Towers|tower]] with a long range capable of damaging enemies with its explosions. It can be unlocked by reaching {{Level|75}} or purchased via a gamepass for {{Robux|300}}. When the player reaches {{Level|75}}, the '''Mortar''' can be claimed by using the pop-up message that appears when joining the lobby.

The '''Mortar''' is a cliff tower that shoots rather slowly but deals high damage over a large explosion radius. It has a long range, but as the '''Mortar''' shoots at a target farther away, the shells take longer to reach their target. These shells will generally land behind the targeted enemy. The explosion it produces is separated into two spheres; enemies in the inner sphere will be dealt 100% of the stated damage, while the damage in the outer sphere decreases linearly. At Level 4, when the '''Mortar's''' shells explode, they will drop three cluster bombs, which will create additional explosions when they hit the ground. These will land around the main explosion, meaning they are very unlikely to all hit and deal full damage to the targeted enemy. By default, the '''Mortar''' can damage [[Lead (Attribute)|Lead]], as well as target all non-hidden flying enemies. The '''Mortar''' is immune to all stuns by default. 

The '''Mortar''' is considered an all-round tower due to its relatively cheap placement cost, expensive upgrade prices, and its ability to effectively handle groups of enemies throughout the game thanks to its high area damage and strong overall damage output.

The '''Mortar''' has a placement limit of four per player. This is increased to five in [[PVP]] mode.

==Strategy==
*The '''Mortar''' is essentially a cliff-based [[Rocketeer]]. However, the '''Mortar''' deals less single target damage without its cluster bombs (which are very unlikely to all deal full damage to the enemy) while having a significantly larger explosion. This makes the '''Mortar''' a better option against hordes.
*In most cases, the '''Mortar's''' shells will land behind an enemy.
**This can be useful for hordes of enemies, as the '''Mortar's''' shells will land closer to the middle of the hordes instead of landing at the front. This allows its explosions to hit more enemies.
**However, this makes the '''Mortar''' somewhat weaker against single targets. As explosions will land behind an enemy, the enemy will be further away from the center of the explosion, which can reduce the amount of damage it receives.
***This is usually negligible for slow enemies, but for much faster ones like the [[Fallen Rusher]], the '''Mortar's''' DPS may be reduced drastically.
*At Level 4, the '''Mortar's''' shells drop cluster bombs when they explode. These cluster bombs will also explode when they hit the ground.
**However, do note that these should not be relied on, as the cluster bombs are very inaccurate and create a smaller explosion than the shells.
**At least 1 or 2 Level 5 '''Mortars''' can be efficient with taking out hordes of [[Breaker4]]s due to it being able to kill it in a single hit and its cluster bombs killing the [[Breaker3]]s that spawn.
*Compared to the [[Ranger]] and [[Sniper]], the '''Mortar''' has a significantly smaller range, lacking in range when cliff spots are far enough from the path.
**The [[DJ Booth]] can aid with this by boosting its range.
*The [[Commander]] can help remedy the '''Mortar's''' slow firerate.
*This tower has surprisingly good DPS for a splash damage tower, so it will also be able to help with major bosses like the [[Fallen King]].
**Additionally, it can easily take care of their summons with its explosions.
*The '''Mortar''' cannot detect [[Hidden (Attribute)|Hidden]] enemies. You will have to use other towers for an effective [[Hidden (Attribute)|Hidden]] defense.
**In most gamemodes, [[Hidden (Attribute)|Hidden]] enemies are usually close to other enemies, so the '''Mortar''' can indirectly hit them with its large explosion radius.
**Towers like the [[Ace Pilot]] or [[Firework Technician]] can give hidden detection but must be close to the '''Mortar''' in order to do so.
*Large clumped groups that appear from [[Breaker4]] can be better targeted by setting '''Mortar''' to ''Random''.
*This tower completely ignores defense, dealing full damage to the enemy. Because of this, it can be useful against hordes of enemies with defense, like [[Fallen Hazmat|Hazards]], [[Reaver]] or [[Fallen Giant]].
*In [[Hardcore Mode|Hardcore]] mode, the '''Mortar''' can be used to kill hordes of [[Lead (Attribute)|Lead]] enemies.
*The '''Mortar''' can be an effective tower in certain maps where you can stack the '''Mortar''' to maximize the cliff spaces and free up more space for other cliff towers like the [[Ranger]].

==Trivia==
*Outside of sales, the '''Mortar''' is a {{Level|75}} tower with a gamepass cost of {{Robux|300}}, lower in Robux compared to the [[Turret]] which is a {{Level|50}} tower with a gamepass cost of {{Robux|340}}.
*The '''Mortar''' is loosely based on the [[w:c:roblox-tower-battles:Mortar|Mortar]] from ''Tower Battles'' and the [[w:c:bloons:Mortar Monkey|Mortar Monkey]] from the ''Bloons Tower Defense'' series.
*When the '''Mortar''' was first released, it would have been affected by shockwaves created by bosses from their ''Stomp'' abilities. This would later be fixed the same day.
**This glitch would later return in the [[Overhaul v1.0|Overhaul Update]], though it only applied to the [[Grave Digger (Legacy)|Grave Digger]]'s ''Stomp''. This was later fixed in the [[v1.1.0|Entity System Update]].
*The '''Mortar''' formerly shared its Level 4 icon with the [[Ace Pilot]]'s Level 2 icon, but rotated.
*The '''Mortar''' has a unique explosion at Level 5. It will create a nuclear explosion, instead of the usual explosion most other splash towers use.
*Unlike the game, dropping a shell in real life will spontaneously fire the mortar. In the game, '''Mortar''' drops in a shell after firing and waits for an enemy to come close to fire again.
*The '''Mortar's''' default skin in the lobby uses its [[Hardcore Testing BETA]] appearance with its face changed, instead of its current appearance.
